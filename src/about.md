Hi, I'm Joanna. Welcome to *Colours of my life* — a place where I write
about Christian living, health, parenting and family, and about overcoming
the challenges life brings.

Life isn't a single shade. It's grief and joy, illness and hope, exhaustion
and excitement — and through all of it, faith gives colour to every season.
I write to process, to remember, and to encourage anyone walking a similar
road.

The easiest way to follow along is the [RSS feed]({{ "/feed.xml" | url }}), or just drop
by from time to time.
